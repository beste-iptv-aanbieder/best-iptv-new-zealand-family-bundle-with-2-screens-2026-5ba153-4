Generic Budget Resellers – Common Risks

These cheap plans promise a lot but often fail. Their servers get overloaded, especially on weekends when many people watch live events. You may see constant buffering or streams that freeze every few minutes. The picture quality is usually lower, and activation can take hours or even days. Many of these sellers ask for payment methods that offer no refunds, and support is slow. For a family that needs reliable TV on two screens, this option is not worth the small savings.

Expat / International Server Lines – Good Variety, High Latency

These services offer a wide range of channels from different countries – great if your family wants content from overseas. However, the servers are often located far from New Zealand, so there is a noticeable delay (latency) between the live action and what you see. Audio can go out of sync, especially on fast-moving programmes. The picture might be 4K at the start but drop quality when the server gets busy. If you can handle occasional glitches and don’t mind a small delay, this could work, but it is not the most stable choice for two screens.

VOD-Only Providers – Great for Movies, Weak for Live TV

These providers focus on on-demand movies and TV series. Their libraries are large, and the picture quality for pre-recorded content is often very good. But when you try to watch live TV – news, sports, or events – the streams are unreliable. They may buffer, freeze, or stop working entirely. For a family that mainly watches movies and shows on demand, this is fine. But if you want both live TV and a reliable two-screen setup, a dedicated live IPTV service like SubTV IP is a better fit.
